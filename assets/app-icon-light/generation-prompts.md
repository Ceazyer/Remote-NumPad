## 1 · 生成

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production launcher icon shared by Remote NumPad Android APK and Windows EXE, one single square icon, not a presentation sheet. Create a beautiful premium LIGHT neumorphic icon: a pale cool off-white rounded-square tile with a precise symmetrical 2x2 cluster of large soft raised rounded-square keyboard keycaps. Top row keycaps show '1' and '2', bottom row show '3' and a simple warm ochre-gold return/enter arrow. Above the four keycaps is a small refined wireless signal mark in muted slate gray, conveying remote input. Large clear thin-to-medium sans-serif dark slate numerals, aesthetically restrained. Orthographic frontal view, no perspective, no tilt. Palette: tile and keycaps #F2F3F7, numerals #242A36, muted #626977, single warm gold accent #99600F; soft pure white top-left highlights and cool pale #D2D5DF lower-right shadows, matte porcelain, subtle tactile depth, no chrome or gloss. Strong simple silhouette readable at small launcher sizes, balanced generous margin, centered content with all identifying marks inside central 60 percent of canvas. Tile fills about 88 percent of square canvas with generous smooth rounded corners; transparent alpha outside tile, no background scene or ground. Only the exact numerals 1,2,3, no wordmark, no app name, no border decoration, no phone mockup, no checkerboard painted into image, no watermark. High quality crisp 1024x1024 square bitmap, exactly one finished icon.
